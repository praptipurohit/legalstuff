==============================================================================
 LEGAL CONTRACTS MIS — MICROSOFT FORMS + POWER AUTOMATE SETUP GUIDE
 For: Reliance Legal Team (Microsoft 365)
==============================================================================

WHAT THIS ACHIEVES
──────────────────
Reviewers fill a web form (on phone, laptop, browser — anywhere).
Power Automate automatically adds their submission to a SharePoint list.
You stop chasing emails and pasting data entirely.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PART 1 — CREATE THE MICROSOFT FORM
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Step 1: Go to https://forms.microsoft.com
        Sign in with your Reliance Microsoft 365 account.

Step 2: Click "New Form"

Step 3: Title the form:
        "Legal Contracts MIS — Monthly Submission"

Step 4: Add a Description:
        "Please submit all new contracts and advisory matters you have
        reviewed this month. Fill one form per contract.
        Deadline: [insert your monthly deadline]"

Step 5: Add the following questions (exact field types shown):

  Q1  Text (Short answer, REQUIRED)
      Label: "Document Name"
      Hint:  "Full name of the contract/document"

  Q2  Text (Short answer, REQUIRED)
      Label: "Vendor / Party"
      Hint:  "Full legal name of the counterparty"

  Q3  Choice (Dropdown, REQUIRED)
      Label: "Business Division"
      Options:
        Enterprise
        Telecom
        Media
        Digital
        Retail
        Corporate
        Other

  Q4  Text (Short answer)
      Label: "Business SPOC"
      Hint:  "Name of the business person who sent you this contract"

  Q5  Choice (Dropdown, REQUIRED)
      Label: "Your Name (Reviewer)"
      Options: [list all reviewer names — same as your Excel dropdown]
        Neel
        Faizan Khan
        Kulwinder Singh
        [add remaining reviewers]

  Q6  Choice (Dropdown)
      Label: "Second Reviewer (if applicable)"
      Options: [same list as Q5 + "N/A"]

  Q7  Date (REQUIRED)
      Label: "Date of Receipt"
      Hint:  "Date you received this contract"

  Q8  Date
      Label: "Date of Reply / Review Completion"
      Hint:  "Date you sent back your comments (leave blank if pending)"

  Q9  Text (Short answer)
      Label: "Contract Value"
      Hint:  "e.g. 50,00,000 or N/A – Advisory"

  Q10 Choice (Dropdown, REQUIRED)
      Label: "Current Status"
      Options:
        Under Review
        Reverted to Counter-party
        Pending with Counter-party
        Pending Execution
        Pending with Business
        Executed / Closed
        Not Responded
        On Hold
        Abandoned
        N/A - Advisory

Step 6: Settings (click the gear icon):
        ✓ "Record name" — ON (so you know who submitted)
        ✓ "One response per person" — OFF (they may submit multiple contracts)
        ✓ "Accept responses" — ON
        ✗ "Shuffle questions" — OFF

Step 7: Share → Copy link
        Send this link to all reviewers each month.
        Tip: Pin it in your Teams channel for easy access.

PART 2 — CREATE THE SHAREPOINT LIST (your new "Master Database")
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Step 1: Go to your SharePoint site (your team's SharePoint, not personal OneDrive)

Step 2: Click "New" → "List"

Step 3: Name it: "Legal Contracts Master"

Step 4: Add columns (click "Add column" for each):

  Column Name          | Type           | Notes
  ---------------------|----------------|----------------------------------
  Document Name        | Single line    | Already exists as "Title" — rename it
  Vendor / Party       | Single line    |
  Business SPOC        | Single line    |
  Business Division    | Choice         | Same options as your form
  First Reviewer       | Person         | Links to your M365 directory
  Second Reviewer      | Person         |
  Date of Receipt      | Date           |
  Date of Reply        | Date           |
  Contract Value       | Single line    |
  Status               | Choice         | Same 10 options as your form
  Submission Source    | Choice         | Options: Form, Manual, Migration
  Submitted By         | Person         | Who entered this record

Step 5: Enable versioning:
        List Settings → Versioning Settings → "Yes" for item versioning
        This is your audit trail — FREE, built into SharePoint.

Step 6: Import your existing 700+ rows:
        From the list page → "Export to Excel" to get the template
        OR: Open the list → click "Edit in grid view" → paste your data

PART 3 — CREATE THE POWER AUTOMATE FLOW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This flow runs automatically when a reviewer submits the form.

Step 1: Go to https://make.powerautomate.com
        Sign in with your Reliance M365 account.

Step 2: Click "Create" → "Automated cloud flow"

Step 3: Name: "Legal MIS - Form to SharePoint"
        Trigger: search for "Microsoft Forms" → select
                 "When a new response is submitted"

Step 4: Configure trigger:
        Form Id: select your "Legal Contracts MIS — Monthly Submission" form

Step 5: Add action: "Get response details" (Microsoft Forms)
        Form Id: same form
        Response Id: [use the dynamic value "Response Id" from trigger]

Step 6: Add action: "Create item" (SharePoint)
        Site Address: [your SharePoint site URL]
        List Name: "Legal Contracts Master"
        Map fields:
          Document Name      ← [Document Name] from form
          Vendor_x002f_Party ← [Vendor / Party] from form
          Business SPOC      ← [Business SPOC] from form
          Business Division  ← [Business Division] from form
          First Reviewer     ← [Your Name (Reviewer)] from form
          Second Reviewer    ← [Second Reviewer] from form
          Date of Receipt    ← [Date of Receipt] from form
          Date of Reply      ← [Date of Reply] from form
          Contract Value     ← [Contract Value] from form
          Status             ← [Current Status] from form
          Submission Source  ← "Form" (type this as static text)

Step 7: Add action: "Send an email (V2)" (Outlook)
        To:      [your email address]
        Subject: "New MIS Submission: " + [Document Name]
        Body:    "New contract submitted by [Responder's Name]:
                  Document: [Document Name]
                  Vendor: [Vendor / Party]
                  Status: [Current Status]
                  Review the SharePoint list to verify."

Step 8: Save and test:
        Click "Test" → fill the form yourself → verify entry appears in SharePoint.

PART 4 — MONTHLY REMINDER FLOW (automated chasing)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Create a second flow:

Step 1: "Create" → "Scheduled cloud flow"
        Name: "Legal MIS - Monthly Reminder"
        Start: 1st of next month
        Repeat: Every 1 Month

Step 2: Add action: "Send an email (V2)"
        To:      [all reviewer email addresses, separated by semicolons]
        Subject: "Action Required: Legal MIS Monthly Submission — [Month]"
        Body:
        "Hi Team,

        Please submit your legal contract updates for [Month] using the form below.

        FORM LINK: [paste your Microsoft Form link here]

        Deadline: [15th of the month] — after which the monthly MIS will be compiled.

        Please submit one entry per contract. For contracts carried forward from
        last month, please submit a status update entry.

        Thanks,
        Legal Team Admin"

Step 3: Add a second "Send an email" action with a delay:
        Before this step, add "Delay" → 14 days
        Send a reminder to anyone who hasn't submitted.
        (To get the "who hasn't submitted" logic, this requires a more advanced
        flow — raise with IT if needed, or do this manually for now.)

PART 5 — CONNECTING SHAREPOINT TO YOUR EXCEL FOR REPORTING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Your LegalMIS_v2.xlsm Master sheet can pull live data from SharePoint:

Step 1: In your Excel file, go to Data → Get Data → From Online Services
        → From SharePoint Online List

Step 2: Enter your SharePoint site URL

Step 3: Select "Legal Contracts Master" list

Step 4: Click "Transform Data" → remove columns you don't need → "Close & Load"

This creates a live connection. Clicking "Refresh All" in Excel pulls the latest
data from SharePoint into your Master sheet automatically.

Your VBA macros (ColorAllSheets, RefreshMonthlySummary) still work on this data.

SUMMARY: THE NEW WORKFLOW ONCE SET UP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  REVIEWER FLOW:
  1st of month  → Reviewer receives automated email with form link
  Anytime       → Reviewer fills form on phone/laptop (2 minutes per contract)
  On submit     → Entry goes directly into SharePoint — no email, no attachment

  ADMIN FLOW:
  Mid-month     → Open Excel → Data → Refresh All → Master updates automatically
  End of month  → Run RefreshMonthlySummary → MIS report ready in 30 seconds
  Ad-hoc entry  → Use Quick Entry sheet for contracts notified verbally/informally

  WHAT DISAPPEARS:
  ✗ Chasing reviewers for submissions
  ✗ Copy-pasting from emailed templates
  ✗ Spelling errors breaking lookups
  ✗ File corruption from conditional formatting
  ✗ "I didn't get the template" emails
